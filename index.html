How to play

  1. Split into 2 or more teams.
  2. On your turn, one player describes 5 words to their team — without saying the word itself, part of it, or a "sounds like" rhyme.
  3. You have 30 seconds. Tap ✓ when your team guesses correctly, or ✗ to skip a word.
  4. Score a point for every word your team guesses. First team to the target score wins!
